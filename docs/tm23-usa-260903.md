# TM23 USA 260903 support

Hyundai Santa Fe (`TM23`, shown on the unit as `TMFL_23`), US, head unit software 260903
(`TM23.USA.0000.V016.010.260903`).

**Status: installs and starts on the car; wireless CarPlay does not complete yet.** One
owner ran this on the car over adb with a USB Wi-Fi adapter. The Bluetooth and iAP2 part of
wireless CarPlay works and the phone joins the head unit's hotspot, but the CarPlay session
never finishes (see "What was tried on the car"). Wireless Android Auto has not been tried.
The off-car checks (`tools/tm23`, the same engine regression the CN7NPE entry uses, on
synthetic stock files) pass.

## Compatibility

`mod.json` and the generated NEMESIS descriptor accept TM23 USA 260903. The patch selector
is limited to `TM23.USA.0000.V016.010.260903`; other TM23 builds, regions and firmware are
not included. The head unit needs an enabled HMG-API hook map for this firmware. This mod
does not install one. The owner's HMG-API 1.1.0-rc.4 Car Report (2026-11-17, platform
`tcc897x`) shows the map probed for Santa Fe (usa 260903), checked on this car and not yet
marked verified: 351 of 351 points found, none missing.

The patch set (`patches/tm23_us/stock`) records four app fingerprints (md5 of the stock
`.odex` files in `/system/app`) from that report (`map_car.files`). No manufacturer app
is replaced. The unit's active one-bin folder is `tm23_us`, and its `featurexml.cfg` has
the three disabled feature lines this mod merges: the hotspot
(`com.hkmc.software.wifihotspot`), wireless CarPlay and wireless Android Auto.

## Wi-Fi

The report shows `wifi_chip: false` and the `hmgapi.wifi.unpowered` feature: no Wi-Fi chip
answers on SDIO. The owner confirms this head unit has no Wi-Fi chip fitted. This mod asks
HMG-API for the hotspot, and for such a mod HMG-API treats an unpowered chip as meeting
`hmgapi.wifi`, so it installs. The built-in hotspot cannot come up on its own: with the
hotspot feature on, the stock Wi-Fi HAL loads `bcmdhd.ko` and no `wlan0` appears.

Wireless CarPlay and Android Auto on this car therefore need a USB Wi-Fi adapter and a
driver built for this kernel (3.18.24-tcc, no stack protector, its own defconfig). The
SG2EV adapter driver is not built for it. The USB adapter mod is kept in its own
repository, because it replaces a system file and only suits units without working Wi-Fi.

## What was tried on the car

With the adapter and a TM23 build of its driver installed (a TP-Link Nano AC600, RTL8811AU):

- The head unit's own hotspot comes up on 5 GHz and a phone and a watch join it.
- Wireless CarPlay pairing over Bluetooth (iAP2) works, and the phone is sent the hotspot
  details and joins the head unit's CarPlay hotspot.
- The CarPlay session does not finish. DiPO starts a wired-session timer about 6 seconds
  after `startCarPlaySession`. When it fires, `resetUSB()` power-cycles the USB port through
  the micom service, which drops the adapter before the wireless session is up. Wired
  CarPlay does not need the adapter, so this never matters there.
- The hotspot name the phone is told is the one in the CarPlay entry of
  `/data/misc/wifi/softap.conf`, which can differ from the hotspot's own name. Until the mod
  builds the hotspot from the CarPlay entry, make the two entries match by hand.
- With the adapter on a head unit that uses the CarPlay firewall setting, set it too (as on
  the Elantra N, see the README), and turn ADB over TCP off: its `eth0` address clashes with
  the hotspot's address range.

The `resetUSB()` timer needs a hook point in HMG-API (or another way around it) before
wireless CarPlay can finish on this car. Audio, turn-by-turn, uninstall and rollback of the
wireless mod on this car are untested.

## What is not known

- Wireless CarPlay and Android Auto end to end on the car, audio delay, turn-by-turn,
  uninstall and rollback.
- Whether the CarPlay firewall setting is needed, as on the Elantra N (README).

An owner who tries it: say the result per feature (CarPlay, Android Auto, firewall setting)
in the pull request or an issue, with the HMG-API version and map status.
